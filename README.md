## 🚀 Sub-Agent Pipeline Breakdown

TrendPilot AI uses an autonomous multi-agent chain to turn a single prompt into a multi-platform content strategy:

* **Trend Discovery Engine:** Analyzes campaign topics to identify viral angles, market trends, and unique audience hooks.
* **Content Copywriter:** Crafts tailored platform captions matching target persona tones (LinkedIn, Instagram, X/Twitter, YouTube).
* **Hashtag Strategist:** Generates niche-specific, platform-optimized hashtag stacks to maximize reach and discoverability.
* **Video Script Writer:** Constructs 30-second video scripts complete with visual cues, audio timing, hooks, and CTAs.
* **QA & Compliance Auditor:** Evaluates tone consistency, readability scores, and platform compliance guidelines before output generation.
* **Markdown Exporter:** Compiles and formats complete campaign blueprints into downloadable `.md` files.
